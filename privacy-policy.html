Privacy Policy — M Notebook

Last updated: 10 October 2026

M Notebook (package com.mnotebook.app, "the app") is a personal note-taking app. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not.

What the app stores

  • Your notes and images. Notes are saved on your device in a local database (offline-first). If you sign in with Google and enable sync, note files (JSON) and attached images are uploaded to your own Google Drive, into a folder named "M Notebook" in your Drive root. The app uses the drive.file scope, which means it can only see and manage files it created itself — it cannot read any other files in your Drive.
  • Google account identity. Signing in stores your email address, display name, and profile photo on the device, used only to label your sync account and to isolate each account's notes. There is no Firebase — sign-in is direct Google Sign-In and Drive API.
  • Your PIN (if you set one). The PIN is stored only on the device as a salted, iterated SHA-256 hash. The PIN itself never leaves your phone and is never sent to Google or anyone else.

What the app does NOT do

  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no trackers. The app ships no Firebase, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party analytics.
  • No contacts, location, microphone, or camera access beyond what you choose. Images come only from photos you pick or attach yourself.
  • No selling or sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. Data sent to Google Drive is covered by Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Your control

  • Sign out in the app to stop syncing; your notes stay on the device.
  • Delete the "M Notebook" folder in Google Drive to remove the cloud copy.
  • Uninstall the app to remove the local database and settings from the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app release it applies to.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the app developer through the app's support channel.
